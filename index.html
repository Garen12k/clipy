Clipy

Edit, caption and post your clips.

Terms of ServicePrivacy Policy

Clipy is a video editor for iPhone. You put clips and photos on a timeline, trim them, and add music, text, captions, stickers, filters, transitions and effects. When your video is ready you can save it, share it, or send it to your own social accounts.

What you can do

  • Cut and arrange clips and photos, with layers and collages.
  • Add music, sound effects and voice-over, and cut to the beat.
  • Add text, captions and stickers, with styles and animations.
  • Apply filters, transitions and effects, and choose the frame shape.
  • Start quickly with a ready-made style.

Posting to your accounts

If you choose to, you can connect your own YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook or X account and send a finished video to it from Clipy. For TikTok, Clipy sends the video to your TikTok inbox as a draft; you add the caption and post it in the TikTok app yourself. Clipy never posts anything unless you ask it to.

Your videos stay yours

Your projects and media stay on your phone. You do not need an account to edit. See the Privacy Policy for the details.
