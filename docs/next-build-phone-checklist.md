# Next build — what to try first on the phone

This is ONE list for the whole next build, in the order that matters. It replaces nothing: the three longer lists stay
(`project-backup-phone-checklist.md`, `undo-history-phone-checklist.md`, `text-size-phone-checklist.md`) and this list points
to them where they go deeper.

## Before you start

- **Install the new build OVER the Clipy you have. Do NOT delete the app first.** Part A is about the projects that are
  already on your phone; deleting the app would delete them.
- Go from the top. Parts A and B are the important ones: they are about not losing your work.
- Under each check, write what you saw. If something looks wrong, stop and tell me the number of the check.

---

## A. Your existing projects (do these before anything else)

### 1. Everything is still there
Open Clipy.
- **Right looks like:** every project you had is on the home screen with its picture and its name. Write down how many.
- **Wrong looks like:** a project is missing, or one says "Can't open" that did not say so before.

### 2. Old projects open
Open three: the newest, the oldest and the longest. Play each. Go back without changing anything.
- **Right looks like:** each opens and plays, with all its clips, texts and sounds. Back on the home screen nothing changed.
- **Wrong looks like:** a black clip, a missing sound, a project that will not open, or a card that changed by itself.

### 3. An edit is kept
Open a project, trim one clip, go straight back to the home screen, open it again.
- **Right looks like:** the trim is there.
- **Wrong looks like:** the trim is gone, or the project says "Can't open".

### 4. A fast leave is safe
Open a project, add a clip with "+", go back within a second, open it again.
- **Right looks like:** it opens and the new clip is there.
- **Wrong looks like:** "Can't open", or the clip is missing.

### 5. Closing Clipy loses nothing
Swipe Clipy away in the app switcher. Open it again.
- **Right looks like:** the same number of projects as in check 1.
- **Wrong looks like:** fewer projects, or a new "Can't open" card.

### 5b. Save to Photos still works
This build saves an exported video to Photos in a new way (so Open Instagram can find it). Export a short project and tap **Save to Photos**. Do it once with Photos allowed, and note what happens if Clipy asks for Photos access first.
- **Right looks like:** "Saved to Photos", and the video is in the Photos app once, playing normally.
- **Wrong looks like:** an error message although the video appears in Photos, no video in Photos, or the video twice.

## B. Saving while you leave the app

### 6. Edits survive a sudden close
Make three edits, swipe up to the app switcher and swipe Clipy away at once. Open Clipy and the project.
- **Right looks like:** the three edits are there. Undo is lit and takes them off one by one; Redo puts them back.
- **Wrong looks like:** an edit is missing, or the project is damaged.

### 7. An interruption in the middle of a drag
Start dragging a clip's edge and, while you hold it, pull down Control Centre (or let a call come in). Come back.
- **Right looks like:** the project is whole. Undo takes it back to before the drag.
- **Wrong looks like:** a broken clip, or Undo does something else.

### 8. Recording your voice the first time
Add audio → Record → tap the button. Apple asks for the microphone: allow it.
- **Right looks like:** the countdown runs, the recording starts, the meter moves. After Stop the voice is on the timeline.
  Leave the project and open it again: the voice is still there.
- **Wrong looks like:** nothing is recorded, or the voice is gone after reopening.

### 9. Leaving during an export
Start an export, leave Clipy for a few seconds, come back.
- **Right looks like:** the export goes on, or starts again by itself, as before. Afterwards the project is unchanged and Undo
  still works.
- **Wrong looks like:** the export screen is stuck, or the project changed.

## C. Save a Copy and Open Project File

(More checks: `project-backup-phone-checklist.md`.)

### 10. After Save a Copy, the home screen still works — the fix of this build
Save a Copy of a SMALL project (one with photos only is the best test). The share sheet appears. Close it. Now tap New
Project. Do this three times in a row; once, try Quick Edit and the folder button instead.
- **Right looks like:** every time, New Project opens your library (Quick Edit opens its styles, the folder opens Files).
- **Wrong looks like:** you tap New Project, Quick Edit or the folder button and NOTHING happens. Also tell me if the share
  sheet did not appear at all after "Saving a copy".

### 11. A copy comes back as a new project
Save a Copy → Save to Files → On My iPhone. Tap the folder button and pick that file.
- **Right looks like:** "… was added to your projects" and a new project at the top with its picture: same clips, text and
  music. It plays and exports. The original is unchanged. The new project's Undo is grey.
- **Wrong looks like:** the original changed, the new one misses something, or nothing was added.

### 12. Closing the file picker without choosing
Tap the folder button, then close the picker (Cancel, or swipe it down). Then tap New Project.
- **Right looks like:** nothing is said, and New Project opens your library.
- **Wrong looks like:** New Project or Quick Edit does nothing afterwards.

### 13. Through iCloud Drive and AirDrop
The same as check 11, saving to iCloud Drive, and sending by AirDrop to another device.
- **Right looks like:** the file on the other side has the full size and opens in Clipy.
- **Wrong looks like:** a tiny file, or "This project file is damaged or incomplete."

### 14. Sending one copy and starting another
AirDrop a copy and, while it is still sending, start a second Save a Copy.
- **Right looks like:** the first file arrives whole.
- **Wrong looks like:** the first file arrives and says "damaged or incomplete" when opened. (This can happen by design —
  tell me if it does, it is easy to make safer.)

### 15. Names
Save a Copy of projects whose names have a space, Arabic letters, an emoji.
- **Right looks like:** the file keeps the name and ends in `.clipy`.
- **Wrong looks like:** a strange or empty name.

### 16. Your biggest project
Save a Copy of the biggest project you have, then open that file. Note the size and both times.
- **Right looks like:** the bar moves all the way; the phone does not lock while the card shows.
- **Wrong looks like:** the bar stops, or the phone locks and the copy stops.

### 17. Cancel half-way
Press Cancel in the middle of a save, and in the middle of an open.
- **Right looks like:** the card goes, nothing is said, nothing new is on the home screen.
- **Wrong looks like:** a card that stays, or a half project on the home screen.

### 18. A file that is not a project
Pick a photo or a PDF with the folder button.
- **Right looks like:** "Can't open this file — That isn't a Clipy project file."
- **Wrong looks like:** anything else, or a new broken project.

### 19. Leaving Clipy in the middle
Leave Clipy in the middle of a save and of an open, and come back. Then once more: force-quit Clipy in the middle of an open
and start it again.
- **Right looks like:** it finishes, or stops with a plain message; after the force-quit there is no "Can't open" card and all
  projects are there.
- **Wrong looks like:** a card that stays for ever, or a new "Can't open" card.

### 20. Count again
After part C: count the projects.
- **Right looks like:** the number of check 1 plus the ones you added, and every old one still opens.
- **Wrong looks like:** any other number.

## D. Undo comes back

(More checks: `undo-history-phone-checklist.md`, items 3–12.)

### 21. Undo after reopening
Three edits → home screen → open the project.
- **Right looks like:** Undo lights up a moment after the editor appears. Three Undo, then three Redo, all work.
- **Wrong looks like:** Undo stays grey, or it undoes something else.

### 22. Redo after reopening
Undo twice → home screen → open.
- **Right looks like:** Redo is lit.
- **Wrong looks like:** Redo is grey.

### 23. Through Export and Post
Two edits → Export → Done → home screen → open. Then the same through Post.
- **Right looks like:** Undo undoes both edits; the project still shows where it was posted.
- **Wrong looks like:** Undo is grey, or the "posted" mark is gone.

### 24. The longest project
Ten small edits in your longest project → home screen → open.
- **Right looks like:** it opens as fast as before, nothing stutters in the first second. Tell me how many Undo steps came back.
- **Wrong looks like:** a slow or stuttering opening.

### 25. Rename and Duplicate
Rename a project on the home screen and open it. Duplicate a project and open the copy.
- **Right looks like:** after the rename, Undo is grey and the project is as it was, with the new name. The copy's Undo is grey;
  the original keeps its steps.
- **Wrong looks like:** Undo brings the old name back, or the original lost its steps.

### 26. Remove background and Undo
Turn Remove background on, wait until it is made, turn it off → home screen → open → Undo.
- **Right looks like:** the Preview tag shows while it is made again, then the cut-out is back. Tell me how long it took.
- **Wrong looks like:** nothing happens, or an error.

### 27. Being interrupted often
In your longest project, make an edit, then pull Control Centre down and up a few times, between edits.
- **Right looks like:** nothing stutters when you come back.
- **Wrong looks like:** a visible pause each time you come back to Clipy.

## E. Export presets

### 28. The tiles
Open Export.
- **Right looks like:** three tiles (two when the project has no 4K clip). Tapping one sets the three controls under it;
  changing a control un-picks the tile, or picks the right one. Pick Message, export, Done, open Export again: Message is
  still picked, and the exported file has that size.
- **Wrong looks like:** a tile picked that does not match the controls, or the choice forgotten.

### 29. A project that never used them
Export an old project without touching the tiles.
- **Right looks like:** 1080p, 30, High — as before.
- **Wrong looks like:** any other setting chosen by itself.

## F. Text size

(The full list, and what sizes A, B and C are: `text-size-phone-checklist.md`.)

### 30. At your normal size (A)
- **Right looks like:** Clipy looks exactly as before.
- **Wrong looks like:** anything smaller, larger or moved.

### 31. The home screen at B and C
Look at the two bottom buttons and the three buttons in the top pill. At B, start a Save a Copy.
- **Right looks like:** every label is whole; the card's title sits beside Cancel; the two buttons come back when it ends.
- **Wrong looks like:** a label cut off, or buttons on top of each other.

### 32. Export's tiles
- **Right looks like:** at A each small line is whole, with no dots; at B it is one line and the tiles are equally tall.
- **Wrong looks like:** dots, two lines, or tiles of different heights.

### 33. The Record tab at B and C
Look at the countdown number and the meter. Cover the microphone until "Too quiet — check the microphone" shows.
- **Right looks like:** the number is inside the ring; the sentence is on one line, or on two with nothing cut. Tell me which.
- **Wrong looks like:** a cut sentence, or a number outside the ring.

### 34. Your video does not change
At C, look at text on the video, export, and compare with A.
- **Right looks like:** the same as at A; the timeline's labels and ruler do not change either.
- **Wrong looks like:** bigger text on the video or in the exported file.

### 35. The rest
A long project name (dots, not tiny letters), the editor not moving, touch-and-hold large labels at C, Post and TikTok
options, Accounts, the welcome pages, typing, and changing the size while Clipy is open — as in the text-size list.

## H. Open Instagram

Only on this new build (the button needs a setting that only a new build carries; on the build you have now it never shows).

### 36. With Instagram on the phone
Export any project. On the finished screen tap "Open Instagram".
- **Right looks like:** a grey "Open Instagram" button with Instagram's mark under Save to Photos. Tapping it says "Saved to
  Photos" (or asks for Photos access the first time) and Instagram opens with your video picked, ready for you to post it
  yourself. If Instagram opens on its home screen instead, write that down: the "open on this video" part is Instagram's own
  and not promised by Meta.
- **Wrong looks like:** no button although Instagram is installed, two copies of the video in Photos after tapping Save to Photos
  and then Open Instagram, or nothing happening at all.

### 37. Without Instagram
On a phone without Instagram (or after deleting it), export again.
- **Right looks like:** no "Open Instagram" button; Post to… / Save to Photos / Share / Done exactly as before.
- **Wrong looks like:** the button is there.

## I. Deleting an account that signed in with Apple

Only once Apple sign-in works (today Apple answers "Sign Up Not Completed"); the server part needs the two secrets of
`supabase/README.md` §14 "Apple: revoking". Use a throw-away Apple ID.

### 38. Apple asks once more, then the account is gone
Accounts → Delete account → Delete.
- **Right looks like:** Apple's sheet appears a moment after the alert; after Face ID, "Account deleted." and the Sign In card.
  In iPhone Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple, Clipy is no longer listed.
- **Wrong looks like:** no Apple sheet, the account deleted without asking, an error from Apple's sheet every time, or Clipy
  still listed in Settings after a minute.

### 39. Closing Apple's sheet
Sign in with Apple again, Accounts → Delete account → Delete, then close Apple's sheet.
- **Right looks like:** "Delete without Apple's confirmation?" with Cancel and Delete Anyway, a moment after the sheet closes.
  Cancel: still signed in. Delete Anyway: "Account deleted."
- **Wrong looks like:** the account deleted the moment the sheet closes, no second question at all (write that down: it
  needs a short wait added), or no way to delete at all.

### 40. Email accounts are unchanged
Sign in with email, Accounts → Delete account → Delete.
- **Right looks like:** exactly as before — no Apple sheet, "Account deleted."

## J. Copy style / Paste style for texts

### 41. Copy a text's look onto another
Make two texts. Give the first a font, a size, a colour, a background and a shadow or glow. Select it: the bar shows Edit,
Animate, Keyframe, Copy style, Duplicate, Add text, then Delete. Tap Copy style, then select the second text and tap Paste style.
- **Right looks like:** "Style copied" after the first tap and Paste style appearing beside Copy style; after Paste style the second
  text wears the first one's look but keeps its own words, place, pinch zoom, turn, alignment and animation, and
  "Style pasted" shows with Undo. Undo (in the message or the top bar) gives the old look back exactly.
- **Wrong looks like:** the words or the position change too, two undo steps, Paste style missing, or no message.

### 42. Captions, stickers, and another project
Select a caption and a sticker; then open another project and select a text there.
- **Right looks like:** no Copy style / Paste style on a caption's or a sticker's bar; in the other project Paste style is there
  and works. After closing Clipy completely and opening it again, Paste style is gone until something is copied again.
- **Wrong looks like:** the tools on a caption or sticker, or Paste style missing in the other project.

## K. New Project and Quick Edit never stay stuck

### 43. Close the picker at once, then tap again
On Home tap New Project (choose "Choose from Library" if the menu shows), and close the photo picker the moment it appears.
Tap New Project again at once. Then do the same with Quick Edit: pick a style, close the picker as soon as it shows, and tap
Quick Edit again.
- **Right looks like:** each second tap opens the menu / the picker / the style sheet again, every time; nothing is created by
  the closed picker; Open Project File and Save a Copy still work afterwards.
- **Wrong looks like:** a button that does nothing until Clipy is closed and opened again, two pickers on top of each other,
  or a project made from a picker that was closed.

## L. Your own Quick Edit styles (Save As Style)

### 44. Save a project as a style
Home → a project's More (or touch and hold its picture) → **Save As Style**. The name box shows the project's name; change it
and tap OK.
- **Right looks like:** Save As Style sits between Duplicate and Save a Copy; for a moment "Saving your style" shows where the two
  bottom buttons are, then "Style saved. You'll find it in Quick edit."
- **Wrong looks like:** no name box, the project itself changed, or the home screen stuck after saving.

### 45. The style in Quick Edit
Tap **Quick Edit**. Then tap your style, Choose Photos and Videos, and pick 5 photos.
- **Right looks like:** "Your styles" above "Styles", with your style's star tile; a long name ends in dots and never gets
  smaller. The draft has the same song, the same filter, the same transition between photos, your title with its look in its
  place, and the photos moving as in the project. The note under the tiles says "Music: <the song>." (or "No music.").
- **Wrong looks like:** "Music: ." with nothing after it, a different song, or the title in another place.

### 46. Your own song, then delete the project
A project with a song of your own (not one of Clipy's) and beats placed: Save As Style, then DELETE that project. Make a Quick
Edit with the style.
- **Right looks like:** the draft still has the song, and the photos change on the beat, each about 2–3 seconds — even if two
  of your beat marks were close together.
- **Wrong looks like:** "This style's music is missing.", or every photo very short.

### 47. Your own song with no beats placed
- **Right looks like:** the draft has the song, photos of about 3 seconds, no beat marks.

### 48. Rename and delete
In Quick Edit, touch and hold your style.
- **Right looks like:** Rename / Delete / Cancel appears ON TOP of the Quick Edit panel. Rename shows the new name at once.
  Delete → "Delete style?" → Delete: the tile goes, and all your projects are still there.
- **Wrong looks like:** the menu hidden behind the panel, or a project gone.

### 49. VoiceOver
- **Right looks like:** "Your styles" and "Styles" are read as headings; your tile says its name and "Touch and hold to rename
  or delete."; a double tap and hold opens the menu.

### 50. A style from an empty project
Save As Style on a project with no song, no filter and no text, then make a Quick Edit with it.
- **Right looks like:** the draft is just your photos, each as long as it would be in a new project.
