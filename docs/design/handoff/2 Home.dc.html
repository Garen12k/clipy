NOTES
Busy: while a project is created, the grid and top buttons are inert and dimmed, and the bottom bar becomes one status capsule with words and the count copied (constraint 16).
Damaged: red border, warning symbol and “Damaged” on the picture; the name in red. Its More menu offers only Delete.
Callout: shown once, under the Post button; one line and a close control. Not TipKit.
Empty state: uses the nautical moment (a sailing symbol in accent ink) and points at the two actions in the bottom bar.
