Editor v2: fixed preview, timeline, toolbar
The preview has one place and one size per device, and no tool changes it. Strips cover only the toolbar and the timeline rows under the clip row. Panels cover only the timeline and the toolbar. On the smallest iPhone the preview’s height is set by the keyboard, so typing doesn’t cover it either.
A1
Layout math · preview height in every state
Top to bottom. Status bar → 44-pt header → preview → 44-pt transport → timeline (fixed cap) → 4 → 64-pt toolbar → bottom inset.
Preview bottom = the lower of (transport top − 4) and (keyboard-with-suggestions top − 40-pt typing bar − 2). The remaining gap above the transport is left empty on purpose.
Strips are bottom-aligned with the toolbar, and their top never goes above clip row + 4. Largest strip: 168 pt, which is exactly that space on the smallest iPhone.
Panels take timeline + gap + toolbar: 240 / 280 / 320 pt. A panel opens and closes without animation. Swipe-down on the header closes it at once; this is off while a voice-over is being recorded.

B1
Timeline · the app’s order, pinned clip row, scrolling rows, 44-pt targets
Order kept from the app: ruler 14 → clip row 52 (pinned) → 4 → Music, Voice, Sound effects → one row per Layer → Text, Stickers, Captions → Effects. The rows scroll vertically inside the cap and show a 3-pt scroll indicator. Summary rows and the gutter are removed.
Lanes are 40 pt with 30-pt bars; each bar’s touch area is 44 pt, reaching 2 pt into the next lane, and the nearest bar centre wins. Each kind keeps its glyph and name on the bar; sound bars add a waveform only when the data exists. Ship-first look: no waveform (left phone).
Handles: dark #1C1C1E tabs with a gold (clip) or white (bar) grip line, 14–16 pt visual, 44 × 44 target (pink). Move: a 32 × 16 dark pill with a gold ring at the top centre of the selected clip; target 44 × 40 (blue).
Cut markers stay visible. At the selected clip’s cuts the “+” or ◆ moves 10 pt outward, beside the trim handle, onto the neighbour clip. Its 24-pt disc wins over the handle’s target (yellow). Markers are hidden only where two cuts are closer than 44 pt; there, Transition is in the Edit group.
Nothing covers anything: top edge 3 pt = copy progress · top-left = badge · top centre = Move · bottom 12-pt band = beat ticks and keyframe diamonds (8 pt, 44-pt target, green). Touch order: trim handle → marker disc → keyframe → Move → clip. On clips under 100 pt the badge hides; under 88 pt Move is a touch-and-hold on the clip.
Multi-select: gold border + gold check on chosen clips, an empty ring on the others. There are no handles or markers.
C1
Toolbar · all contexts at 375 pt
Group button: a 52-pt tool-sized button with the group’s symbol over “Basics ⌄”. It opens a system menu (Basics, Edit, Audio, Look, Frame) with a checkmark on the current group. VoiceOver: “Tool group, Basics, pop-up button.” At 375 pt four tools are visible beside it, as in the app today.
Basics holds eight tools, no tool appears twice. Basics: Split, Trim, Speed, Volume, Filter, Cut out, Stabilize, Select. Edit: Transition, Keyframe, Duplicate, Replace, Reverse, Freeze. Audio: Extract audio, Voice, Sound. Look: Adjust, Templates, Animate (+ Motion on photos). Frame: Crop, Transform, Opacity, Mask, Background, Green screen (layers: Blend instead of Background).
One rule: the group resets to Basics on every new selection. Flat rows: main bar, empty project, audio, text, text / caption / sticker, sound bar (opens on Volume, Fade), effect, multi-select (“✓ 3 selected” in 13 pt, then Speed, Volume, Filter visible, Delete and Done pinned).
