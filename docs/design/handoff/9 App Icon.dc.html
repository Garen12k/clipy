CLIPY · iOS 27 · REVIEW 3 · F10 · SUPERSEDES THE PLAY-BUTTON ICON
App icon · a compass
A compass rose inside a ring. The north point is gold and the south point cream, so it reads as a compass at every size, and nothing in it looks like a play button. It is made of four flat layers for Icon Composer, with no highlights, shadows or blur of our own.
