R2
Confirmation banner · in the transport row, never on the video
New feature
Place. The banner takes the 44-pt transport row, which sits between the preview and the timeline, so it covers no video and no control the person is using. No shadow. Two lines of 13 pt, then one action. It works the same with the toolbar, a strip or a panel open.
How long. Until the next edit, or until Play. Tapping Play (the person’s next likely action) dismisses it and brings the transport back at once. Undo stays in the transport row as before. VoiceOver announces the sentence.
Actions: Undo (success), Try Again (failed action), Open Settings (permission), Show (a background copy failed: opens its strip). “Show” New feature
R3
Keyboard states at 375 × 667 · preview stays 301 pt
With the keyboard up, the only room left is the 40-pt typing bar above it. Each tool puts its field there. Emoji results scroll sideways in the bar; Return or dragging the panel shows the full grid. Fine-tune moves field to field with the arrows.
R4
Editor under accessibility settings, and glass vs solid
Timeline, toolbar, one strip and one panel in each case. Increase Contrast: 1.5-pt borders in label colour, bars and clips outlined in black, accent ink #F0CE8A (light #6E4A00), secondary labels at full contrast. Bold Text: every label one weight up. Reduce Transparency equals the solid version. Text inside strips grows with Dynamic Type up to × 1.16 (the largest standard size); above that it stops, and the large-content viewer shows a tool or tile’s name and symbol on touch and hold.
R5
Blur box and Mosaic box on the preview
Selecting a Blur box or Mosaic box effect puts its rectangle on the preview: white border with a black edge, four corner handles (44-pt targets), drag to move. It disappears when the effect is deselected.
R6
Permission rows · every permission × every state
One row component, used on wizard page 3 and in Accounts → Permissions, in dark and light. The restricted state always reads “Managed on this iPhone”. Limited carries both actions.

R8
Notifications · one card, and what Export shows after
New feature
The card appears on the export progress, only while notifications have never been asked. Grey “Continue” opens Apple’s alert. No “Not Now”: the card simply stays until the export ends. It promises nothing about leaving the app.
