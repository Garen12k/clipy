Every sequence is 5 seconds or less and plays once when its page becomes current, then rests on its last frame; it replays if the page is revisited. The green-outlined frame in each row is the Reduce Motion still. Easing: the system’s standard spring (no bounce) for moves, ease-out for fades. Page changes use system paging. VoiceOver: one description per illustration, plus “Page 2 of 4”. At large text sizes the illustration shrinks first, then the page scrolls.
6a
Permission flows · in context
1 · Ask once, in context. The wizard only offers. Anything skipped is asked at first use. Never two system alerts in a row, and never again right after a refusal.
2 · Explanation first, unless obvious. An inline card with a headline, one sentence and “Continue” (never “Allow”, no reward, no second Cancel). The record button goes straight to the system alert.
3 · After a refusal. The feature shows its inline state with “Open Settings”, not a toast. Returning from Settings updates the row and the feature at once, with no restart message.
