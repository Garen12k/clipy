# Text size: what to check on the phone

Clipy now follows the iPhone's text-size setting, up to a limit, so that large text never breaks a screen. None of this has been seen on a real phone yet. This list is what to look at, most important first.

If something looks wrong: take a screenshot and note which size (A, B or C) the phone was on.

## Where the setting is

- **Settings → Display & Brightness → Text Size**: a slider with seven steps.
- For the biggest sizes: **Settings → Accessibility → Display & Text Size → Larger Text**, switch on **Larger Accessibility Sizes**, and the slider gets longer.

You can leave Clipy open while you change it. Come back to Clipy and it should already show the new size.

## The three sizes to try

| | How to set it | What you should see |
|---|---|---|
| **A** Normal | Text Size slider on the middle step (the fourth of seven). Larger Accessibility Sizes off. | Clipy exactly as it looked before. |
| **B** Top of the normal slider | Text Size slider all the way right. Larger Accessibility Sizes off. | Outside the editor, words are clearly bigger. Inside the editor, words are only a little bigger. |
| **C** The largest of all | Larger Accessibility Sizes on, slider all the way right. | Almost the same as B. Only the paragraphs on the welcome and sign-in pages grow further. |

Start at A and check that nothing changed. Then do the list at B, then at C.

## What to look at

### 1. Home: the two buttons at the bottom

Quick Edit and New Project sit side by side.

- Right: both buttons are fully on the screen at A, B and C, with the same gap from the left and right edges. At B and C the words may be the same size as at A rather than bigger. That is intended; there is no more room.
- Wrong: New Project is cut off by the right edge of the screen; the two buttons touch or overlap; a label is so small it is hard to read; a label breaks onto two lines.

If you have an older or smaller iPhone, this is the one to check on it. It is the tightest place in the app.

### 2. Home: a project with a long name

Rename a project to something long, for example "Summer holiday in Portugal with the kids 2026".

- Right: under the picture, the name is on one line and ends in three dots. The letters are bigger at B than at A.
- Wrong: the whole name is squeezed in using tiny letters.

Also look at the words on the picture itself (the cover title, and the length such as 0:42): they must look exactly the same at A, B and C.

### 3. The editor's top bar, with that long name

Open the long-named project.

- Right: the name at the top ends in three dots; Back and Export are in their usual places.
- Wrong: tiny letters; the name pushing Export off the screen.

### 4. Text on the video, and the exported file

At size A, add a text and a sticker to a video. Notice how big they are compared with the picture. Switch to C and come back.

- Right: the text and the sticker are exactly as big as at A. Export the video at C: the text in the exported file is the same size, in the same place, as in the editor.
- Wrong: the text on the video is bigger at C; the exported video does not match what the editor showed. Check a caption with highlighted words too, if you have one.

### 5. The editor: nothing moves

Compare A with B and C.

- Right: the video and the timeline are in exactly the same place and the same size at all three. The toolbar's labels are a little bigger at B and C and still on one line.
- Wrong: the video or the timeline is shifted or smaller; a tool's name ends in dots where it did not at A; words overlap an icon.

Open a few tools: **Filter**, **Speed** (all three tabs), **Volume**, **Trim**, **Remove background**, then the tall ones: **Text** (type something), **Stickers**, **Add audio**, **Captions**.

- Right: every label is readable and inside its row; the names under the small picture tiles (filters, masks) are not cut at the bottom.
- Wrong: a letter's tail (g, y, p) cut off under a tile; a label overlapping a switch or a slider.
- Fine, not a fault: a longer note or status sentence inside a tool ending in three dots at B and C.

### 6. The timeline

- Right: the names on the coloured bars, the small badges on clips (the filter's name, the speed) and the numbers on the time ruler look exactly the same at A, B and C.
- Wrong: any of them bigger at B or C.

### 7. Touch and hold for a large label (size C only)

At C, touch and hold each of these without letting go: **Back**, **Undo**, **Redo**, **Play**, **Aspect ratio** (the button that shows 9:16 or similar), then a tool in the toolbar and a button on Home.

- Right: the control's name appears large in the middle of the screen. Let go: the large name goes away. A normal quick tap still works as before.
- Wrong: nothing appears; two large labels appear on top of each other; a quick tap stops working.
- Please note for us: when you let go after the large name appeared, did the button also do its action? Either answer is useful. Try it on **Delete account**'s row on Accounts in particular, and press Cancel in the question that follows if it opens.

### 8. Export

- Right: the three choices (resolution, frames per second, quality) each on one line; the buttons readable. Start an export: the percentage inside the ring is the same size at A, B and C.
- Wrong: a choice cut off; the percentage spilling out of the ring.

### 9. Post, and the TikTok options

Open Post from Home, then TikTok's options.

- Right: each platform row shows its name and its action (Connect or Disconnect) without overlapping; rows get taller if they need to. A connected account with a long name ends in dots. The TikTok options sheet stays inside the screen and scrolls to its last row.
- Wrong: an action pushed off the right edge; the sheet running off the bottom so the last rows cannot be reached; an account name in tiny letters.

### 10. Accounts

- Right: your email address on one line, ending in dots if it is long. Sign Out and the red **Delete account** row fully visible. The Build row and the Background export row readable.
- Wrong: the email in tiny letters; Delete account cut off or overlapping its icon.

### 11. The welcome pages

On Accounts, tap **Show welcome again**.

- Right: at C the paragraphs are noticeably bigger than at B, and still smaller than the title above them. Every page scrolls far enough to reach its buttons. The four permission rows are readable. On the last page, the email step and the code step both fit, with the keyboard up.
- Wrong: a button you cannot reach by scrolling; text running under the dots at the bottom; the Sign in with Apple button cut off.

### 12. Typing

At C, type in a text field: a new text in the editor, the email on the sign-in page.

- Right: what you type is the same size as the words around it (limited, not huge).
- Wrong: the typed text or the grey hint is far bigger than everything else, or cut off inside its box.

### 13. Changing the size while Clipy is open

With Clipy open on Home, switch from A to B in Settings and come back.

- Right: Home already shows the bigger words and the two bottom buttons are both on screen.
- Wrong: nothing changes until Clipy is closed and opened again.
