Privacy Policy

Clipy - last updated 10 October 2026

HomeTerms of Service

In short: your projects, photos and videos stay on your phone. Clipy only stores something on its server if you sign in and connect a social account, and then only what is needed to post for you. Clipy has no ads and does not sell your data.

What stays on your phone

Your projects, the photos and videos you add, your music, recordings, captions and exported videos are stored on your iPhone. Clipy does not upload them to its server. Automatic captions, where available, are made on the phone by Apple's speech recognition.

If you sign in

You can use the editor without an account. If you sign in (with email, Apple or Google), Clipy's server stores your email address and a user ID so it can recognise you. Sign-in is handled by Supabase, the service that runs Clipy's server, and sign-in emails are sent through an email delivery service.

If you connect a social account

When you connect YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook or X, you sign in on that platform's own page; Clipy never sees your password for it. The platform gives Clipy access tokens limited to what posting needs. Clipy stores these tokens encrypted on its server, together with the account's public name and ID, so that it can send videos when you ask.

  • What Clipy does with the access: it sends the video you chose, with the title, caption and options you entered, to the account you chose - only when you press Post. For TikTok, the video is sent to your TikTok inbox as a draft, and you finish the post in the TikTok app.
  • What Clipy does not do: it does not read your messages, followers, or other posts, and it does not post, like, follow or comment on its own.
  • Where the video goes: your phone sends the video to the platform. For some platforms it passes through Clipy's server on the way; it is not kept there after the upload.

Use of YouTube features is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Each other platform's own terms and privacy policy apply to what you post there. Clipy's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

  • Disconnect an account: in Clipy, open Accounts and disconnect it. Clipy deletes the stored tokens for that account. You can also remove Clipy's access in that platform's own settings (for Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions).
  • Sign out: in Clipy, open Accounts and sign out.
  • Delete everything on the server: ask through the contact below and your account, with its stored tokens, will be deleted.
  • Delete what is on your phone: delete projects in Clipy, or delete the app.

Permissions on your phone

Clipy asks for access to your photo library to add photos and videos and to save exports, to the camera to take a photo or video for a project, to the microphone to record a voice-over or the sound of a video you take, to speech recognition to make captions, and to notifications to tell you when an export has finished. You can change these in the iPhone's Settings at any time.

Analytics, ads and sharing

Clipy has no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking. It does not sell or rent your data. Data is shared only with the services needed to do what you ask: the server provider, the email delivery service for sign-in codes, and the social platform you choose to post to.

Children

Clipy is not directed at children under 13, and the social platforms have their own age rules.
